# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

Reports: **atedgimo@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer, handled
confidentially, and answered — enforcement ranges from a warning to a
permanent ban at the maintainer's judgement.
