Turn 4 · light theme

Light theme

Not an inversion. On white the three verdict colours have to be darkened to stay legible, and the surface stack flips direction — chrome goes lighter than content instead of darker. Below: the token mapping, then 1a rebuilt in it.

The dark theme’s #3DD68C / #F5B544 / #F2545B fail on white — amber worst, at 1.9:1. The light set runs 4.6–5.9:1 as text and keeps the same hue order, so a screenshot in either theme reads identically.

One structural change: in dark, elevation reads as lighter; in light it reads as whiter with a border. Do not port the dark theme’s filled tiles onto light — use white cards with a 1px border and no shadow.

Same capabilities as your current build — plan header, Green/Yellow/Red triage, step list, Safety Guard verdicts, recommendations, rehearsal, history, the four cluster views — rebuilt around the one job: an SRE deciding whether to approve this plan. Your build sits at ~1.6:1 contrast in places (grey load bars on near-black). Every pair here clears WCAG AA: body text 8.9:1, secondary 5.2:1, all three verdict colours ≥ 4.6:1 on surface.

What changed, and why
One primary action, not threeRun / Dry run / Run to optimum competed as equals. Now: one filled button reflecting the actual selection, rehearsal as its sibling, optimum demoted to a menu.
The step list is the screenIt was a 320 px sidebar showing 4 of 14 steps. It is now the main object, grouped by verdict, with per-step Safety Guard reasoning.
Red explains itself inline“7 held back” was a number with no path to the reason. Every held-back step now names the rule and the fix.
